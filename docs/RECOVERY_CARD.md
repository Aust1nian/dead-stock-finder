# Dead Stock Finder: recovery card (for salespeople)

One page. The owner installs on **their own computer**, from tightline.systems/deadstock. Never use a USB stick, and never install from your laptop.

## Installing

**Mac** (macOS 11 or later)
1. Open the downloaded file and drag **Dead Stock Finder** into Applications.
2. Open it from Applications. The first time, macOS asks once whether to open an app downloaded from the internet: click **Open**.
3. After setup, macOS shows a one-time notice that **"Dead Stock Finder" added a background item**. That's the daily check. Leave it on.
4. If it won't open: right-click the app in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open** again. If there's no Open button, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway**.

**Windows** (10 22H2 or 11, 64-bit)
1. Open the downloaded file and follow the installer. It never asks for an administrator password; if anything asks for one, stop and contact a founder.
2. A blue **"Windows protected your PC"** box can appear for new downloads. Windows signing isn't settled yet, so don't improvise: contact a founder.

## Square's approval screen

1. In the app, the owner clicks **Connect Square**. Square's own page opens in their browser.
2. They log in with **their** Square account. Square always asks, even if they're logged in already, so the right account gets connected.
3. Square lists what the app asks for: read the business profile, items, orders and inventory, and **write items** (only to add the "Last sold" label).
4. They click **Allow**. The app comes back to the front and says "Connected to *their business name*". If it shows a different business, they used the wrong login: Settings → Disconnect Square, then connect again.
5. The app's name on Square's page is the company's Square application. If the page shows an error instead, take a photo and send it to a founder.

## What the owner will see

- The first sync reads up to 12 months of sales and can take up to 10 minutes. After that, the daily check takes under a minute.
- Labels appear in the **Square Dashboard**, on each item's page, as "Last sold Jul 14, 2026". **Square's point-of-sale app doesn't show them.** That's Square's choice, not a fault.
- The app needs no window open: it checks once a day and at each sign-in. The computer must be on and signed in for labels to stay current; while it's off, labels keep their dates, so they stay true.

## When something goes wrong

- **"Reconnect Square to keep labels current."** Square ended the connection. This happens after about 80 to 90 days with no check (for example, a computer left off), or if someone removed the app in Square. Open Dead Stock Finder, click **Connect Square**, and approve again. Nothing is lost.
- **"Checks have failed 3 days in a row."** Usually no internet at check time. Labels in Square stay as they are. Open the app and click **Check now**. If it fails again, use **Help → Save diagnostics** and send the file to a founder. Item names and amounts are removed from it.
- **"Dead Stock Finder stopped writing labels."** A safety stop: Square stored more than a label on one item, often because someone edited that item at the same moment. Open **Settings**, read what changed, and choose **Resume labels**. If the owner is unsure, send a founder a photo.
- **"Square won't add the Last sold attribute."** Square allows 10 custom attributes per account and this one is full. The report still works; labels need one free slot in Square Dashboard → Items → Settings → Custom attributes.
- **Is it working on this computer?** Open the app, then **Help → Check this computer**. If it says something isn't working, take a photo and send it to a founder. Don't keep troubleshooting on the owner's machine.
- **Removing it.** First **Settings → Remove all labels from Square** (if they want the labels gone), then **Settings → Disconnect Square**. Then:
  - On a Mac, drag the app to the Trash.
  - On Windows, open Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Dead Stock Finder → Uninstall.

  To cut Square off from the app too, use **Square Dashboard → Settings → App integrations → Disconnect App**.
